# WiData observed benchmark

Direct interactive visits: homepage; HPG; TCB; SSI; VHM; HPG and TCB Financial reports; TCB financial indicators; methodology with bank efficiency and bad-loan sections; industry entry. See screenshots/ and ../validation/BROWSER_ACCEPTANCE.md.

Company identity, compact valuation/size metrics, issuer website, auditor and related peers sit above the tabs. HPG has industrial analysis templates, TCB banking templates, SSI securities templates, VHM residential-property templates. Preview images establish template themes; their hidden calculations were not inspected. The Documents tab appeared disabled in these samples.

Financial reports open a dense overlay. Directly observed controls include consolidated-report selection, balance/income/cash/notes/ratios/peer destinations, reverse chronological columns, quarter/year selector, VND billions units, period paging, row expansion, and Excel export. An export button's presence is not an export or redistribution permission. No export was performed. HPG and TCB tables have materially different rows; bank balances include interbank placements, customer loans/deposits and issued funding instruments. TCB indicators expose annual/quarterly growth, TTM efficiency, and a latest-period label. Displayed bank EV/EBITDA was explicitly unavailable.

The methodology groups general, non-financial, bank, securities and insurance models. Bank tables disclose definitions, units, available frequencies, references, revision-history links and a stated 24–48-hour post-publication update objective. This objective was not independently measured. Seventeen bank definitions were read from tables; CASA is separately documented in a public article. METRIC_CATALOG.csv preserves this distinction. No WiData numbers were ingested into Woofi.

Company vs industry: company statements/ratios and the industry overview table are directly observed. The industry page exposes a level-three industry selector, series checkboxes, YTD/3M/6M/1Y/2Y/3Y/5Y/10Y controls, and eleven columns: SMG, day/week change, capitalization, EPS, P/E, P/B, ROA/ROE TTM and PAT YoY for quarter/TTM. Their exact sector aggregation formulas, constituent coverage and historical membership vintages were not certified. Valuation histories require their own share/price vintage. Disclosures and corporate actions remain separate evidence families. Woofi does not infer real-estate project revenue, securities margin/FVTPL economics or insurance ratios from generic balances.
