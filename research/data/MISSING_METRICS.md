# Missing or unqualified

All sampled VCI observations lack qualified consolidation, original units and publication timestamps. The adapter's audited VND ×1 engineering convention is retained; missing original unit metadata stays missing. Matching unknown bases cannot certify a ratio. No sampled metric is newly qualified for public display.

Missing normalized inputs include banking earning-asset/funding breakdowns, demand deposits, loan grading and provision conventions; securities margin loans, FVTPL, brokerage/IB and funding-cost lines; real-estate advances/project disclosures; split-adjusted weighted shares, parent equity, dated capitalization and corporate-action-adjusted per-share bases. No inferred NIM/CASA/NPL, EPS, P/E or P/B is enabled. Reported basic EPS remains a source-period value; TTM EPS is not a sum of quarterly EPS.

The new engine supports eight additional analytical outputs and three supporting averages, but real-source qualification is separate from formula tests. Industry totals require known fiscal boundaries and reviewed membership, which these samples do not provide.
