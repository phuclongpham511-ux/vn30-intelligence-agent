# Observed financial dashboards

Nonfinancial/real-estate overview uses stacked assets and funding with percentage overlays, profit composition with parent PAT, operating/investing/financing cash flows plus closing cash, cost composition and revenue/earnings with growth. PNJ valuation has P/E/P/B histories, averages and capitalization; changing 17 to 9 periods changed the average-history label to 2.1 years. Vendor averaging and adjustment formulas were not disclosed.

Bank overview substitutes lending/interbank/investment assets and customer/interbank/funding liabilities. Profit components include interest, services, investments, FX and other income, with pre-provision and parent profit. Cost charts include provisions, operations and CIR. Growth/risk has YEA/COF/NIM, ROA/ROE, NPL/LLR/NPL2, loan-quality composition, maturity plus LDR, deposit composition plus CASA, loan industries and borrower types.

Securities overview has profit components FVTPL/AFS/HTM/brokerage/lending/services, brokerage profitability, margin versus equity and proprietary investments. Growth adds revenue/PAT/YoY, ROA/ROE, investor NAV/deposits, FVTPL/AFS/HTM asset compositions, and a proprietary-stock portfolio table with cost/market prices, quantities, book/market amounts and unrealized profit. The table distinguishes an update date from quarter-end date; neither was reconciled to original disclosures.

VHM growth exposed revenue/parent profit, YoY, ROA/ROE and margins. No project cash-flow forecast or revenue-recognition formula was directly established. Generic charts must not be interpreted as verified property-specific disclosures.

Industry peer position allows company selection and a YoY overlay. Ranking is a dense, horizontally scrollable table with issuer links, ranks, capitalization/liquidity, EPS/BVPS/multiples, returns, revenue/PAT and growth/margins. The inspected ranking displayed update 06/08/2026, not the visit date. Retail impact returned no data. Valuation compares current multiples to three-year averages. No aggregate P/E formula or coverage denominator was established.
