# Industry and specialist observations

Direct industry tabs: overview, peer position, stock ranking, industry impact, industry valuation. Retail overview exposed the same assets/funding/profit/cost families as issuer charts. Five initial peer series were MWG, PNJ, FRT, DGW and PET. Enabling revenue YoY added growth series for those peers. This default selection does not establish full-sector coverage.

Ranking columns: overall/financial/business rank, capitalization, MA20 share volume, EPS, P/E, P/B, BVPS, ROE, ROA, net revenue, parent PAT, revenue YoY, gross/net margins, parent-PAT YoY, inventory QoQ, and fixed-assets-plus-CIP QoQ. Retail ranking displayed nineteen rows. Valuation adds current and three-year-average P/E/P/B plus differences. A selector exposed 42 classifications; no stable taxonomy version or effective-dated membership source was supplied by these pages.

Bank metrics directly observed on TCB: NIM, COF, YEA, CIR, ROA/ROE, NPL/LLR/NPL2, loan loss provisions and quality groups; short/medium/long loans and LDR; demand/term/escrow/special-purpose deposits and CASA; credit/deposit growth; lending by industry and borrower legal type. CAR appeared in the bank header. Exact denominators, averaging and regulatory bases were not disclosed. Woofi's accounting loan/deposit comparison must not be labelled equivalent to regulatory LDR.

Securities directly observed on SSI: margin loans/equity and ratio; brokerage profit/margin; investor NAV/deposits; profit and asset compositions by FVTPL/AFS/HTM, listed/unlisted equities, bonds and deposits/certificates; proprietary holdings and unrealized gain. No investment-banking or funding-cost formula was certified.

Property samples VHM/KDH/NLG used general financial chart families. Customer advances, project delivery and project revenue recognition were not qualified by those charts. Insurance existed in the industry selector; no insurer financial page was deeply audited.

All labels and references are in METRIC_CATALOG.csv and DETAILED_LABEL_RECEIPTS.json. Formula and industry aggregation fields remain explicitly undisclosed where the UI did not explain them. No SStock financial numbers or classification universe were imported into Woofi.
