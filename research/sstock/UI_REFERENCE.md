# UI observations and independent Woofi decisions

SStock uses a persistent market header, issuer context, main company/fundamental/technical tabs and secondary task tabs. Shared period count, quarter/full-year and year controls reduce repetitive configuration. Financial tables retain a wide row-label area and chronological period columns. PNJ had nine annual columns 2017–2025 and quarterly Q3/24–Q3/26; Q3/26 cells were dashes. The selected future/current period is therefore not evidence that a statement exists.

Charts are paired dense panels with series legends, mixed amount/percentage axes, image-download and full-screen buttons. Only the presence of download controls was studied; no financial export was performed. PNJ table unit was Tỷ; per-share EPS is a separate unit. Some row buttons were visible, but their expansion semantics were not verified.

Industry views reuse the same selectors and provide nested table scrolling, selectable peers/columns and source update labels. The actual retail impact empty state was “Chưa có dữ liệu”. Hover content can expose amounts and percentages; no formula tooltip was established. Company header source attribution says WiData, while documents link to tailieu.sstock.vn.

Woofi retains its own logo, typography, VI/EN translation and component architecture. It reuses its existing annual/quarterly/TTM control and canonical chart contract, adds four targeted panels and a coverage/peer comparison page with explicit methods, dates and exclusions. It does not reproduce SStock artwork, scores, ranking method or proprietary portfolio dataset.
